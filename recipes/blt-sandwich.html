BLT sandwich

Description

The BLT sandwich is a timeless favorite that combines crispy bacon, fresh lettuce, and juicy tomatoes between two toasted slices of bread. The mix of salty, crunchy, and fresh flavors makes it incredibly satisfying. It’s easy to prepare and takes only a few minutes from start to finish

Perfect for a quick lunch, picnic, or light dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 3 slices of bacon
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • A few lettuce leaves
  • 1 teblespoon mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon in a pan until crispy, then set it aside on a paper towel.
  2. Toast the bread slices until golden brown.
  3. Spread mayonnaise on one side of each slice.
  4. Layer the lettuce, tomato, and bacon on one slice.
  5. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  6. Top with the second slice of bread, cut in half, and serve.
