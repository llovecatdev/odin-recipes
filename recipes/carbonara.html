Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

Spaghetti Carbonara is a classic Italian dish known for its creamy texture and rich flavor. Made with just a few simple ingredients, eggs, cheese, and crispy bacon, it’s comforting yet elegant. The sauce comes together in minutes using the heat from the pasta, without any cream.

It’s the perfect choice for a quick but delicious weeknight meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g bacon or pancetta, chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup granted Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente, then drain (save a little pasta water).
  2. In a bowl, whisk together eggs and Parmesan cheese.
  3. Cook the bacon in a pan until crisp.
  4. Add the hot spaghetti to the pan and remove from heat.
  5. Quickly stir in the egg mixture, adding a splash of pasta water to make it creamy.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper.
  7. Serve immediately with extra cheese on top.
