Banana Bread

Description

This banana bread is soft, moist, and full of natural sweetness from ripe bananas. It’s the perfect way to use up overripe fruit and fill your kitchen with the warm aroma of cinnamon and vanilla.

Enjoy it for breakfast, as a snack, or with a cup of coffee. It tastes great on its own or topped with a bit of butter, honey, or peanut butter.

Ingredients

  • ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/3 cup melted butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar (you can use brown sugar for a richer flavor)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease a loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until smooth.
  3. Stir in the melted butter.
  4. Add the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract — mix well.
  5. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture, then stir in the flour until just combined.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan.
  7. Bake for 50–60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
